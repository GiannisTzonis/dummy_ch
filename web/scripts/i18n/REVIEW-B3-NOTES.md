# Batch 3 review: points for every language (in addition to REVIEWER-BRIEF.md)

The glossary was extended after the translators started; apply the current version. In particular:

1. **Choir headings and list lines with a place** ("Choir X, Timisoara, Romania",
   "… Larissa, Hellas"): the choir name stays exactly as listed; the town and country after it are
   written the target language's way (Grèce / Grecia / Griechenland / Grecia for "Hellas"). The
   checker now protects only the name before the first comma. Translators had kept whole 2022
   headings in English because the checker used to require it: translate their places now, the
   2022 and 2024 lists alike.
2. **Times: 24-hour clock everywhere** ("04:00 - 08:00 p.m." -> "16:00 - 20:00"; "8:30 p.m." ->
   "20:30"), written as in the published batches. The checker now accepts the converted range.
3. **Archive terms** (glossary section "Archive pages"): acoustic rehearsals, open-air / pre-festival
   concert, results announcement, Marking – Medals / Prize Money / Special Prizes, the five marking
   criteria, non-competitive, Repertory requirements, free choice of key, sponsor labels.
4. **Two reviewers per language**: you correct only your own packets, but read the other half's
   `.out.json` files too, so recurring terms, labels, headings and names read the same in both
   halves (and as in the published `<lang>/b1.json` and `b2.json`).
5. Source defects already known (keep as the English has them unless noted): stray control
   characters U+0002 inside some English words (the translation writes the clean word); "41st …
   counts 40 years" / "40th … counts 39 years"; 2023 "5-9 July" vs "6–9 July"; "Toruň"; a Greek
   flag on a Portugal line; "Rustic chivalry [Cavalleria rusticana]" may be given once by its
   usual title.
