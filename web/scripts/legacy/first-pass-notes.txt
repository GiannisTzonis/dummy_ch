Notes written during the first migration pass (Aug 2026), saved from the headers of the
content data files before they were regenerated verbatim on 2026-10-10. Many describe
how content was squeezed into the old structured schema (no longer true); the notes on
legacy-page quirks (wrong years, copy-pasted results, EN/EL mismatches) are still accurate
and are the source for MIGRATION.md's "Old-site errors" list.
