Dear colleagues and friends, We are pleased to inform you that the 2nd International Choral Composition Competition – Preveza 2025 has been successfully completed.
A total of 45 works were submitted by composers from 16 countries, which confirms the strong international interest in our festival as well as in new choral music. We are now delighted to cordially invite you and your choir to our next major event:
44th International Choral Festival of Preveza 30th International Choral Competition of Preveza Preveza, Greece – 9–12 July 2026
The competition includes categories for:
  • - mixed choirs
  • - equal-voice choirs (female / male)
  • - youth and children’s choirs
  • - sacred and contemporary music
  • - senior choirs (60+) with free repertoire and optional accompaniment
All participating choirs will receive a diploma and detailed feedback from the international jury.

The best choirs will be awarded gold, silver, or bronze medals, and selected performances will be published on the official YouTube channel of the festival.
Within the framework of the 44th International Choral Festival of Preveza, we will also announce in the coming days a new cycle of masterclasses for choral conductors.
For the full regulations and application information, please contact us by email or visit our website.
