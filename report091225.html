The “Armonia” Choir in Two Concerts in Thessaly and a Thank-You Note to the General Hospital of Preveza

The Mixed Choir of Preveza “Armonia” in two concerts in Thessaly and a thank-you note regarding the event of the General Hospital of Preveza

The Mixed Choir of Preveza “Armonia” completes a particularly creative ten days of musical activity, continuing its appearances away from home, immediately after its participation in the event of the General Hospital of Preveza on breastfeeding.

Thank-you note for the event in Preveza

On Sunday, December 7, 2025, at the Theofaneios Art Hall, our choir had the honor of providing musical accompaniment for the event organized by the Obstetrics Clinic and the Pediatrics Department of the General Hospital of Preveza, under the theme:

Priority to breastfeeding – Creating sustainable support systems” (WABA 2025)
    “Armonia” expresses its warmest thanks:
  • - to the Administration, the Nursing Directorate, the doctors, midwives, and nursing staff of the General Hospital of Preveza,
  • - to the Medical Association of Preveza for its patronage,
  • - to the Municipality of Preveza for its support,
  • - as well as to all citizens who honored the event with their presence, sending a strong message in favor of breastfeeding and family support.

Participation in the 22nd Tyrnavos Music Festival – International Choir Festival

On Saturday, December 13, 2025, the Mixed Choir of Preveza “Armonia” will participate in the 22nd Tyrnavos Music Festival – International Choir Festival, organized by the Cultural Association of Tyrnavos at the Municipal Theater of Tyrnavos, starting at 19:00, with free admission.

The festival features the following participants:

  • Academic Female Choir of the Students Cultural Center of Nis (Serbia)
  • Mixed Choir of Preveza “Armonia”
  • Vocal Ensemble of the Municipality of Kalamaria
  • Children’s Choir of the Cultural Association of Tyrnavos
  • Mixed Choir of the Cultural Association of Tyrnavos

Our choir will present a program featuring works by Greek and international composers, in a festive atmosphere, representing the city of Preveza in yet another important cultural event.

Participation in the Thessaly Choir Festival – Larissa

On the following day, Sunday, December 14, 2025, the Mixed Choir of Preveza “Armonia” will take part in the Thessaly Choir Festival, which will be held at the Concert Hall of the Municipal Conservatory of Larissa, starting at 11:30.

The festival participants are:

  • Children’s Choir of the Municipal Conservatory of Larissa
  • Mixed Choir of Karditsa “PARENTHESIS”
  • Choir of Preveza “Armonia”
  • “CHÓRES”

With these two performances in Thessaly, “Armonia” continues its path of extroversion and collaboration with prominent choral ensembles from Greece and abroad, while also highlighting the rich cultural life of Preveza.
