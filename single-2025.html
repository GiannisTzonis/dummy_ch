43rd International Choral Festival of Preveza

  • 10 - 13 July 2025

Mark your calendar for 2025 to be part of the 43rd International Choral Festival of Preveza. The main festival will be held from 8 to 13 of July 2025 in Preveza International Choral Composition Competition of Preveza

The International Choral Festival of Preveza counts 43 interesting and exciting years of high-quality choral music, challenged by excellent choirs which are continuously raising its standards and a great variety of intercultural experiences with participants from all over the world. The festival includes numerous concerts given at idyllic locations of the area like the seaside fortress of Preveza and the historic Ancient Auditorium of Nikopolis “Odeon”. For visiting choirs, Preveza provides an opportunity not only to perform; but also, to experience some of the world’s best choral music, soak up the atmosphere and enjoy the mingling, singing and fun, and explore the picturesque city of Preveza and its surrounds. The festival will be managed by the Choral Society "Armonia" of Preveza, under the patronage of the city of Preveza. Choirs are eligible to join the festival as non-competitive choirs participating in all the performances of the festival.

It will be a great honour and joy for us if you come to our beautiful town and take part in the choral competition. We invite you to find out by yourselves that Preveza is a charming and hospitable town full of historical sites and locations of natural beauty and experience the magic of choral music.

Closing date for receipt of completed applications is 1 May 2025.

Ambrož Čopi

Ambrož Čopi (born 1973) studied composition under the tutelage of Dane Škerl, Uroš Rojko, and piano at the Academy of Music in Ljubljana. He is recognized as a composer, conductor, lecturer, and the artistic director of the International Youth Choirs Choral Festival Aegis Carminis in Koper. Presently, he is a leader of choral activities at the Conservatory of Music and Ballet Ljubljana (KGBL) and teaches at the Artistic High School in Koper.. From the inception of his teaching career, he has fostered his students' creativity in choral music, emerging as a mentor to Slovenia's youngest generation of composers. Through his leadership, new compositions have been inspired and performed worlwide. His original compositions, recorded on five CDs, regularly grace stages worldwide. Published predominantly by Astrum Music Publisher, with select works featured by Sulasol, TICF and Hinshaw Music. Čopi has guided numerous Slovenian choirs to remarkable achievements in both national (15 gold plaques) and international competitions (29 first places). He was manytimes awarded for outstanding performances and as best conductor. Presently, he conducts the KGBL Chamber Choir and the University of Primorska Academic Choir, both esteemed among Slovenia's premier choral ensembles. In 2014, he was honored with the Gallus Plaque, Slovenia's highest recognition in the realm of musical endeavor, alongside the prestigious national award, the Prešeren Fund Prize, Mrian Kozina Prize and Republic of Slovenia Prize for Education.. He is regulary invited to act as a jury member at choral competitions worldwide, he actively engages in choral music seminars and masterclasses for conductors and composers alike, offering his expertise as a lecturer.

Angeliki Ploka

Angeliki Ploka is a dynamic Greek choral conductor with a flourishing international career. She has led ensembles and choirs in Greece, Hungary, Spain, Ireland, and the Netherlands, where she is currently based. In the Netherlands, she serves as Artistic Director of The Hague Vocal Ensemble and Kamerkoor Lux. A member of the TENSO team of young conductors, she has collaborated with some of Europe's top vocal ensembles, including Cappella Amsterdam, the Helsinki Chamber Choir, and the Norwegian Soloists’ Choir. From 2020, Angeliki took on the role of Chief Conductor of the Greek Radio Choir, where she introduced innovative programming and memorable performances. Despite the limitations of the COVID-19 pandemic, she created new avenues for the ensemble through both live concerts and broadcasts for radio and television. She is a faculty member at the Royal Conservatoire The Hague and teaches at its School for Young Talent, where she instructs students in music theory, aural training, analysis, and choral singing, all grounded in the Kodály methodology. Angeliki is in frequent demand as a jury member at choral competitions and as a guest teacher in workshops and masterclasses across Europe, focusing on conducting, voice, and music education. As a vocalist, Angeliki has performed with esteemed groups such as the Dutch Radio Choir, Meesters & Gezellen, and Orkest de Ereprijs, under the baton of renowned conductors including Daniel Reuss, Sigvards Klava, Michael Gläser, and Bernard Haitink. She studied singing, choral conducting, pedagogy, and musicology at the Royal Conservatoire The Hague, the Liszt Academy in Budapest, and the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki. She also holds diplomas in piano and theory and has been honored with prestigious awards, including the Sarolta Kodály Scholarship, the A. Onassis Foundation grant, and scholarships from the State Scholarships Foundation of Greece.

Camilo Santostefano

Camilo Santostefano is a distinguished Argentinian conductor of choirs and orchestras, composer, professor, and international jury member. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Choir Conducting from the National University of the Arts in Buenos Aires, as well as degrees in Music Education and Guitar from the Conservatory of Morón. He pursued postgraduate studies in 20th-century Latin American Music Interpretation at UNCUYO University. In recognition of his influence on Argentine music, he received the 2019 Konex Award for Choral Conducting. Camilo is currently the conductor of the Coro Polifónico de Córdoba, the professional choir of the Teatro del Libertador San Martín in Córdoba. He is also the founder and conductor of MusicaQuantica Voces de Cámara, a renowned ensemble awarded internationally, including Grand Prix at Ave Verum (Austria, 2012) and Florilège Vocal de Tours (France, 2012), and finalist at the European Grand Prix (Italy, 2013). He has appeared as guest conductor for ensembles in Wales, Colombia, the USA, and Austria, and previously conducted the Carlos López Buchardo Choir, Lírica Lado B Opera Company, and the National School of Buenos Aires Choir. Between 2019 and 2022, he resided in Austria, leading groups such as the Gumpoldskirchner Spatzen, Melange Vocal Wien, and others. As a composer, he has won national and international prizes, including at the OperaVista Chamber Opera Composition Competition in Houston. He regularly presents masterclasses and conferences across the Americas and Europe. Camilo serves on juries for major international competitions, such as the IFCM-affiliated events in France, Croatia, and the Bahamas. He is a passionate promoter of Latin American choral music and a respected educator, having taught at the Conservatorio Astor Piazzolla in Buenos Aires. His work bridges continents, styles, and generations through music.

Dr. Yu-Chung Johnny Ku

Dr. Yu-Chung Johnny Ku is one of Taiwan’s most influential choral conductors and educators. He currently serves as Music Director of the Taipei Philharmonic Chorus and the Taipei Philharmonic Chamber Choir, as well as the Taipei International Choral Festival and Competition. He is also conductor of the Dream Lotus Chorus, an executive board member of the Taiwan Choral Association, and Assistant Professor at both the National Taiwan University of Arts and Fu-Jen Catholic University. Additionally, he is a member of the World Choral Council, the Asia-Pacific Working Committee of the International Federation for Choral Music (IFCM), a co- founder of the World Choral Championship, and a founding director of the Asian Choral Association. Dr. Ku is an active and versatile conductor, working not only with choirs but also with orchestras, operas, musical theatre, and pop concerts. He has presented concerts, workshops, lectures, and masterclasses in more than 20 countries, including Australia, Austria, Bulgaria, Canada, China, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Indonesia, Italy, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, the Philippines, Poland, Russia, Singapore, Türkiye, and the USA. With the Taipei Philharmonic Chamber Choir, he has been invited to perform at prominent international events such as the ACDA National Convention, BAM’s Next Wave Festival, the Melbourne Festival, and the 2023 World Symposium on Choral Music in Turkey. As Chorus Master, Dr. Ku has collaborated on more than 50 opera and choral- symphonic productions with the National Symphony Orchestra, National Taiwan Symphony Orchestra, and the National Kaohsiung Center for the Arts, working with conductors like Frieder Bernius, Charles Dutoit, Helmuth Rilling, and others. He is a frequent juror in international competitions such as the World Choir Games, Bali International Choir Competition, Singapore International Choral Competition, and many more. Dr. Ku holds a D.M.A. from the University of Cincinnati and a master’s degree from Temple University.

Dr. Eduardo García-Novelli

Dr. Eduardo García-Novelli is a distinguished choral conductor and educator, currently serving as Director of Choral Studies at the University of Kansas. There, he oversees the university's choral program and leads its graduate choral conducting program. Prior to this role, he was Director of Choral Activities and Chair of the Music Department at Carthage College in Wisconsin, where he led the Carthage Choir to national and international acclaim. Under his leadership, the choir performed at multiple state conventions, completed five European tours, and gave a debut concert at Carnegie Hall. In 2019, the choir won both categories at the prestigious Spittal International Choir Competition in Austria. Dr. García-Novelli previously taught at Lamar University in Texas, earning the Faculty Merit Award and achieving landmark performances with the university’s choir, including a first-ever selection for the TMEA Convention and NCCO National Conference. He also served as Artistic Director of the Master Singers of Milwaukee and held leadership roles in South American and U.S. choral organizations. His international experience includes conducting engagements, masterclasses, and adjudication in Europe, China, and the Americas. Highlights include appearances at the World Choral Expo in Portugal, Voce Magna in Slovakia, and a major competition win with the University of Kansas Chamber Choir at the 2023 Preveza International Choir Festival in Greece, where he was also named “Best Conductor.” He holds undergraduate degrees in choral conducting and music education from Argentina, a master’s degree from Westminster Choir College, and a D.M.A. from the University of Houston. Dr. García-Novelli is also active as a church musician and clinician. His work continues to shape the future of choral music through education, performance, and international collaboration.

Josu Elberdin Badiola

Born in 1976 in Pasaia (Gipuzkoa, Spain), Josu Elberdin began his musical training at the Pasaia Musikal Conservatory, earning teaching degrees in piano and voice. He also holds a degree in Social Education from the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU). Since 2000, Elberdin has served as a music teacher at the Pasaia Municipal School of Music and, since 1991, as organist at the Church of Nuestra Señora del Carmen in Trintxerpe. A sought-after clinician, he leads choir conducting workshops for children and adults across Spain and internationally—in countries including Indonesia, Lithuania, France, Bulgaria, and Colombia. He is also a frequent adjudicator at national and international choral and composition competitions. Elberdin is the Technical Director of the Ourencanto Choral Festival and a member of the Technical Committee of the Tolosa International Choral Competition. Renowned worldwide for his choral compositions, Elberdin has received numerous awards and commissions from distinguished choirs. His works have been featured as required repertoire at major international festivals and competitions, including the Tolosa International Choral Competition, Taipei International Choral Festival, Europa Cantat Junior, World Choral Symposium (Argentina 2011 and Barcelona 2017), Quincena Musical de San Sebastián, and Musikaste. His repertoire focuses primarily on music for children's choirs, often presented as musical tales or symphonic stories. In addition to choral works, his catalogue includes symphonic pieces, pop-rock songs, and instrumental compositions. Elberdin’s music is published by Walton Music, Helbling, and CM Musical Editions. His full catalogue is available at www.elberdin.com.

Paweł Łukaszewski

Paweł Łukaszewski (b. 1968 in Częstochowa, Poland) is a leading figure in contemporary Polish sacred and choral music. Often regarded as a successor to composers such as Górecki, Penderecki, Pärt, and Tavener, he is internationally recognized for his deeply spiritual and emotionally resonant compositions. Łukaszewski studied cello and composition at the Fryderyk Chopin Music Academy in Warsaw, graduating with honors in 1995. Since 1996, he has been a faculty member at the Fryderyk Chopin University of Music, where he holds a Ph.D. in composition and the academic title of Professor. In 2016, he was appointed Vice- Rector of the university and has also taught internationally, including in Chile and Argentina. He has received numerous accolades for his work, including the First Prize at the Warsaw Music Academy Competition (1994), multiple national and international awards, ten Fryderyk Awards, and high state honors such as the Commander’s Cross of the Order of Polonia Restituta. His music has been performed in over 100 festivals worldwide and recorded on more than 150 albums by prominent labels such as Hyperion, DUX, and Signum Records. Łukaszewski’s works are published in Poland, the UK, Germany, Italy, and the United States, and over sixty of his compositions have been widely distributed. He is Artistic Director and Conductor of the Musica Sacra Choir at the Warsaw-Praga Cathedral and heads the Musica Sacra Edition publishing house. He also serves as President of the Musica Sacra Association and sits on several artistic and academic councils. In recent years, his music has gained significant attention in the UK, with performances by acclaimed ensembles such as the BBC Singers, Tenebrae, and Polyphony, under conductors like Stephen Layton and Nigel Short. Two acclaimed recordings of his work have been released by Hyperion.

Roni Sugiarto

Roni Sugiarto is one of Indonesia’s foremost choral conductors, renowned for his work with children’s and youth choirs. As Music Director of the Saint Angela Choir (SAC), he has led the ensemble to national and international acclaim. Under his baton, SAC has achieved remarkable success, winning prestigious awards in Hangzhou, Rome, Florence, South Korea, Tokyo, Rimini, and Tolosa. Most notably, SAC won both the Children’s Choir Category and the Grand Prix at the 5th Tokyo International Choir Competition, and in 2024, earned the title of World Choral Champion in Rimini, Italy, along with the Grand Prix at the 4th International Bandung Choir Festival. Roni’s musical foundation was shaped by mentorship from Joseph Kristanto and Avip Priatna. In 2009–2010, he was awarded a scholarship from the German Government (Goethe-Institut) to participate in the Conducting Workshop for Orchestra in Bangkok under the direction of Maestro Gudni A. Emilsson. His international experience includes performing with the Indonesia National Youth Choir (2005) and the World Youth Choir, touring Milan (2006), South Africa (2007), and Hong Kong–Guangzhou–Macau (2008). Dedicated to professional growth, Roni has participated in masterclasses with esteemed conductors such as Mark Anthony Carpio, Jonathan Velasco, Julie Spittill, Marcus LaPratt, William Lock, and André de Quadros. Currently, he serves as Music Director of the ITB Student Choir and the Saint Angela Choir, and as Artistic Director of both the Saint Angela Children and Youth Choir Festival and the Satya Dharma Gita International Choir Festival. In addition to his conducting and artistic leadership, Roni is an experienced adjudicator in choir competitions and regularly presents workshops and training programs for choirs and young conductors throughout Indonesia and abroad.

Yana Deliradeva

Yana Deliradeva is a distinguished Bulgarian choral conductor, widely recognized for her innovative work with children’s choirs and her dedication to music education. She studied choral conducting in both Bulgaria and the United States, earning her master’s degree at Westminster Choir College in Princeton under the mentorship of Prof. Joseph Flummerfelt. Upon returning to Bulgaria in 2002, Yana joined the renowned Detska Kitka Choir in Plovdiv as Assistant Conductor. Since 2011, she has served as the choir’s Chief Conductor, leading over 120 singers from more than 30 schools in the city. Under her direction, the choir continues its legacy of artistic excellence while embracing a contemporary and inclusive approach to repertoire and performance. Her vision includes audience development through interdisciplinary collaborations and the promotion of new music. Beyond her choral leadership, Yana Deliradeva is active in academic and pedagogical circles. She is Assistant Professor of Choral Conducting at the Academy of Music, Dance and Fine Arts in Plovdiv and works as a vocal coach for the Kids’ Academy of the Plovdiv State Opera. A passionate educator, she supports the professional development of young conductors and promotes modern, holistic rehearsal practices. Yana has led conducting workshops in the United States, Sweden, and Bulgaria, and is the recipient of several prestigious awards in recognition of her artistic achievements and contributions to the choral field. Currently, she is pursuing a doctoral dissertation titled “Implementation of Body Methods in the Rehearsal Process with Children Choirs”, reflecting her commitment to integrating movement and physical awareness into choral pedagogy. Through her work, Yana Deliradeva continues to shape the future of Bulgarian choral music and inspire the next generation of musicians and conductors.

New Jersey, USA
Conductor: Dr. Hannah Carr

"Cantigas" Women’s Choir is a non-profit organization based in Hudson County, New Jersey, comprised of women from diverse backgrounds who share a love for singing. Founded in 2002, Cantigas presents two major concerts annually, along with community outreach performances in support of refugees, senior centers, and women’s prisons. The choir’s repertoire features contemporary and often lesser-known composers such as Leonardo San Juan, Paul Hennessee, Lillian Redl, and Wassim Ibrahim, whose «Damascus Breeze» premiered with «Cantigas» in 2015. In 2016, the choir performed in Buenos Aires with sister choirs Coral Femenino de Caballito and Coral San Cirano. «Cantigas» programs often center around hidden voices, including unpublished folk songs. To ensure authenticity and cultural depth, the choir collaborates with cultural informants and guest instrumentalists, enriching both performances and workshops. Their approach has been featured at ACDA and Kodály conferences. Greek Canadian pianist Erasmia Voukelatos has accompanied the choir since its founding and teaches at Far Brook School. Introduction


The International Choral Festival of Preveza is an institution of high artistic quality, both in the Greek and in the international choral field. More than 400 choirs from all over the world have been on its stage, while great personalities have been accredited to its International Jury Committee with their presence, guaranteeing with their glory for the artistic status of the festival as well as the reliability of its procedures.


Participation


All kinds of choirs can participate in the non-competitive festivities of our festival. Working on the direction of its greater spread worldwide, we would like to inform that you will all find out an attractive destination that manages to combine both high artistic value and quality, as well as the advantages of a beautiful tour, as our region is full of archaeological sites from the Ancient Hellenic period and areas of great natural beauty, too. Besides, staying in Preveza could be an opportunity both for day trips to the surrounding area and excursions to the greater area of our precinct.

Organisation


Participating non-competitively gives choirs the opportunity to present informal performances in various locations across the city, while having the possibility to meet with other choirs, see some of Preveza’s beautiful countryside and attend all the festival’s manifestations. Non-competitive choirs are free to organize their own tourist activities while here but are asked to please keep the organizing committee informed as their itinerary comes together to ensure that no time-tabling cross over occurs. Different kinds of performances could be organised in cooperation with the participants. Opportunities to perform in different Greek cities (Lefkas, Ioannina, and Agrinio) are possible to be organised after choirs ask.

The place and the time of the non-competitive concerts will be arranged by the Organizing Committee that will also choose which choirs will participate in each concert. Pieces of each choir's choice (with a preference for sacred or folklore works) will be performed "a cappella" within 10-15 minutes.
